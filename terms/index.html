What Paper Trader is

Paper Trader is a trading simulator. Every order in it is filled with virtual money against real market prices. The app holds no money, has no connection to any exchange order gateway, and cannot place a real trade even if you ask it to.

We are not a broker, not a portfolio manager, not an investment adviser and not a research analyst, and we are not registered with SEBI as any of those.

Connecting your broker

The app can display live market data by connecting to your own broker account using an access token you generate. Three things follow from that, and they matter:

  • The data is yours, under your agreement with your broker. We do not licence, purchase, resell or redistribute exchange data. What you see is delivered by your broker to you.
  • Your token never leaves your phone. It is held in the Android keystore and is not uploaded to us. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Your broker's own terms still apply to that account, including any limits on how their data may be used. Using Paper Trader does not change or override them.

AI features

Some features can turn a sentence into a strategy, or comment on a position, using an AI model. Where these need a key, it is your own key, entered by you. AI output is generated text. It is frequently wrong, it is not reviewed by a human, and it is not advice. Treat it as a starting point for your own thinking and nothing more.
