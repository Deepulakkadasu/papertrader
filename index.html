What it does

  • NIFTY and BANKNIFTY options and futures — the real chain, real expiries, real lot sizes.
  • Margin that behaves like a broker's. Long options are fully paid; writing an option blocks margin; MIS squares off at the real cutoffs. If a trade would be refused at a broker, it is refused here.
  • Brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange and SEBI charges are all deducted. The P&L you see is after costs, which is the only P&L worth practising against.
  • Fills cross the spread and walk the order book. A practice fill at the last traded price teaches the one lesson a simulator must not teach.
  • Charts you can trade from — drag a stop, pull a target out of your position line, place a limit at a level.
  • A trade journal that keeps score honestly, including the trades you would rather forget.

Your broker token, and what happens to it

You are being asked to paste a broker access token into an app you downloaded from a link. That deserves a straight answer, so here is exactly what the app does and does not do with it.

It never leaves your phone

The token is stored in Android's Keystore-backed secure storage on your device. It is never sent to any server of ours — we do not run a server that market data or tokens pass through. It is excluded from Google cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer, so it does not travel even when your other app data does.

No real order can be placed

The app calls exactly four kinds of endpoint at your broker: quotes, historical candles, the option chain, and the market feed. There is no order-placement, funds, holdings or positions call anywhere in the source. This is not a policy we promise to follow — the code to place an order does not exist.

Your data goes only where you send it

Market prices are drawn on your screen and discarded. Nothing is stored or shared with anyone else, and no other user can see anything of yours.

If you choose to sign in with Google to back up your practice trades, your name, email and trading history are stored in your own record so they come back on a new phone. That is optional, and the token is never part of it. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What we can see

If you sign in: your name, your email, and your practice trading history. Nothing else. We cannot see your real positions, your real funds, or your broker password — the app never asks for them and the token only permits reading market data.

Getting it

Android only, and not on the Play Store yet. Android will warn you before installing an app from outside the store, and it is right to. Only install this if you trust the person who sent you the link — and check the fingerprint below matches, so you know the file was not altered on the way to you.

Download the APK

Android 8 or newer. About 22 MB. Pick papertrader-arm64-v8a.apk — that is nearly every phone made since about 2018. Only try the armeabi-v7a file if that one refuses to install.

Check what you downloaded

Every release lists a SHA-256 fingerprint. On a phone, an app like Hash Droid will compute it; on a computer:

sha256sum papertrader-arm64-v8a.apk

If it does not match the one on the release page, do not install it — tell us instead.

Installing

  • Open the downloaded file. Android will ask whether to allow installing from this source — allow it for your browser or file manager.
  • Play Protect may show a warning because the app is not from the store. Choose Install anyway if you are sure of the fingerprint.
  • Turn the permission back off afterwards if you like. The app does not need it again.

Questions

Is there an iPhone version?

No, and not soon. This is an Android app. Everything on this page applies to Android phones only.

Do I need a broker account?

No. Everything works on a built-in practice feed with no account, no token and no payment. You only need a broker if you want the app to show real live prices.

Will it place a trade in my real account by mistake?

It cannot. The app contains no code that places an order.

What happens when my token expires?

Broker tokens usually last a day. The app renews it silently when it can, and tells you plainly when it cannot, so prices never quietly go stale without you knowing.

Do you store my broker password?

We never see it. You paste a token that your broker generates; it permits reading market data and nothing else.

Can I get my money back?

Yes — within 7 days of paying, for any reason. See above.
